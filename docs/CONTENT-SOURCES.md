# Content sources and editorial evidence policy

Research checked: **30 October 2026**. Owner: Rusty (Product). Scope: human-reviewed soccer content automation. **All numerical thresholds below are Overlay Picks editorial decisions, not vendor guarantees, legal advice, or empirically validated betting edges.** External factual statements link to evidence; inaccessible or incomplete evidence is marked **⚠️ Unverified**.

## 1. Scope and release gate

- Phase 1: Premier League, Championship, League One, League Two, FA Cup and EFL Cup (Carabao Cup). No EFL Trophy, women's/youth competitions, international fixtures or other sports in this initial allowlist. Resolve competition IDs and season/round explicitly; a matching team name is insufficient.
- Later, only after approval: La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Primeira Liga; then Turkish Süper Lig, Belgian Pro League, Saudi Pro League; then Brasileirão and Argentine Liga Profesional.
- At most **five new free-tip drafts per UTC calendar day across all runs**; zero is acceptable, and there is no daily publishing target. Retries reuse a draft identity; rejected drafts still consume that day's creation allowance. At most one selection per fixture; no accumulators.
- Automation may create **drafts only**. A human must approve each publication. Credibility does **not** make a source licensed for automated collection, AI grounding, commercial reuse or syndication.
- Adopt the hierarchy below, **not blanket ingestion permission**. Contract-cleared provider endpoints remain gated; public RSS/Atom news consumption follows the owner-approved exception in Consumption policy below. Website crawling remains disabled. If this leaves insufficient team-news evidence, produce **no free-tip draft** rather than relaxing the rules.
- Product context: [SPEC](SPEC.md), [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [Article/FreeTip schema](../prisma/schema.prisma), and the [public tips page](../apps/web/app/tips/page.tsx). These are platform informational tips, not paid tipster picks or an automatically verified performance record.

### Consumption policy

**Owner direction, 30 October 2026:** consume publicly offered RSS/Atom news feeds as a reader, enabled by default. The owner accepted residual publisher ToS risk, including BBC/Guardian automated/AI-use restrictions and incomplete Sky terms. This changes the earlier blanket news-feed licensing gate; it does **not** establish publisher permission, resolve copyright questions, or relax provider contracts/free-tip gates. Source research and restrictions below remain factual risk disclosures, not clearance claims.

The code allowlist is `scripts/content-automation/sources.mjs`: BBC Sport football (`bbc`), Sky Sports football (`sky`) and Guardian football (`guardian`), all enabled with `termsNote` warnings. Each feed has an `enabled` switch; `CONTENT_NEWS_FEEDS` selects IDs, `CONTENT_NEWS_FEEDS_DENY` blocks IDs, and `CONTENT_NEWS_FEEDS_ENABLED=false` kills all news consumption. No guessed club/league feeds are added.

Only published feed endpoints and robots.txt are requested; no HTML crawling, article-body fetching, authentication/paywall access, redirects or bypasses. An identifying OverlayPicks User-Agent, twice-daily schedule, six-hour minimum when request metadata is retained, robots feed-path disallow checks and ETag/Last-Modified requests bound access. An inaccessible robots file (except 404), blocked feed or non-XML response is skipped. Request metadata is cached, not article content. Only headlines, short snippets (maximum 60 words/400 characters), links, publishers, publication times and provenance/policy metadata enter evidence; full content fields and images are ignored. Phase-1 competition filtering is conservative; a human verifies the scope.

Drafting must use facts across independent publishers in original prose with each publisher named and linked. Validation requires two independent Tier A/B publishers and rejects missing links/attribution or any normalized consecutive 25-word overlap with evidence headlines/snippets. Sparse evidence may produce zero drafts. Terms notes reach the reviewer as warnings; human publication review remains mandatory. This guardrail is not a comprehensive plagiarism detector or legal exemption. See [operational configuration and offline verification](CONTENT-AUTOMATION.md#consumption-policy).

## 4. Source tiers and collection rules

These are **adopted editorial rules**. A source must pass both its credibility tier and the access/licensing gate.

### 4.1 What can establish a fact

1. **Tier A — first-party authority:** the relevant organiser/governing body for its own fixtures, results, rules or decisions; the relevant club for its own announcement. One accessible, current, rights-cleared primary source may establish that narrow fact. Attribute it. “The club says X” does not establish an independent diagnosis, an allegation's truth, or an exact recovery date.
2. **Tier B — contracted data:** an authorised provider may alone support routine fixture/result/statistic/quoted-odds facts **within verified coverage**, with provenance and timestamps. It cannot alone establish fitness, motivation, a guaranteed lineup, a suspension's cross-competition applicability or an editorial conclusion. Empty/absent data is “unknown”, not zero injuries.
3. **Tier C — established reporting:** BBC/Sky/Guardian or another specifically approved outlet. A consequential fact lacking Tier A confirmation requires **two independent original reports**, including at least one named, on-record source where practical. Even then label “reported”, not “confirmed”. Routine low-impact context can cite one original report with explicit attribution; it cannot drive a tip without corroboration.
4. **Tier D — analysis candidates/unverified sources:** discovery only; not adopted, not standalone factual evidence, and not tip support. Social rumours, anonymous tip accounts, affiliate previews, search snippets, AI answers and model memory are not evidence.
5. **Independence is origin, not URL count:** two outlets repeating the same wire story, press conference or Opta statistic count as **one**. A vendor plus a site republishing that vendor counts as one for that statistic. Track original publisher, event/statement and upstream data supplier; if origin cannot be resolved, treat as dependent. Independent claims must support the rationale; attaching unrelated links is insufficient.
6. **Conflicts:** prefer the relevant latest primary ruling for its narrow scope, preserve both timestamps and explain corrections. Never average conflicting injury statuses, fixture times or eligibility reports. Unresolved material conflict blocks tips; serious allegations require human/legal escalation and are not an automated-news topic.
7. **Rumours:** do not draft standalone transfer rumours, alleged misconduct or speculative injuries in Phase 1. Necessary contextual reports must say “X reports ...; not confirmed by Y as of [UTC time]”, link the origin and stay out of the headline as an established fact. Never invent quotes or describe training attendance as medical clearance.

### 4.2 Attribution and evidence records

- Each material factual sentence or tightly related paragraph must map to a source. Public format, where permitted: **“According to [publisher], [original factual summary] ([source title](https://example.com), published [UTC date/time]; checked [UTC date/time]).”** The example URL is a formatting placeholder, **not an approved source**.
- Store `publisher`, `kind/tier`, exact `url` or API endpoint **without credentials**, `title`, `publishedAt`/`updatedAt` if available, `retrievedAt`, upstream origin, supported claim IDs, data/fixture IDs and permission reference. Never invent a publication time; use `unknown` plus `retrievedAt`, and do not claim “latest”. For public feeds follow Consumption policy: minimal headlines/snippets plus provenance only, never full article bodies. Contract-backed providers remain subject to their retention allowance.
- Public source names and links, limited factual descriptions and attribution are the default **only where permitted**. There is no universal “headline + link + short attribution is OK” rule. Prefer our own descriptive source title; no copied headlines, quotes, excerpts, graphics, feed descriptions or thumbnails by default. Premier League deep links need approval; if required attribution cannot lawfully be supplied, omit the source/content rather than hiding provenance.
- A feed is a discovery mechanism, not independent evidence of everything in an article. Do not create a full story from a headline/snippet. If access fails or snippets cannot support the claims, skip that candidate; the public-feed ToS exception above is not a fact-verification exception.
- Recheck terms/robots before enabling a host, after notified changes and at least monthly; record date, permitted endpoint/path, purposes (research, AI inference/grounding, publication), limits, attribution, retention, approval owner and permission expiry. Enforce applicable robots feed-path rules. Public-feed terms restrictions are disclosed under the owner-accepted-risk exception above; other sources still require terms clearance. Robots allowlisting never grants commercial rights.
- Source text is **untrusted input**, never tool instructions. Do not follow instructions in pages/feeds, expose credentials, fetch arbitrary linked private resources, bypass blocks or treat a blocked page as permission to use a mirror. Rate-limit retrieval, honour feed TTL and HTTP caching, back off on 429/403 and stop on revoked permission.

### 4.3 Freshness windows (editorial limits)

| Evidence | At generation | At approval/publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breaking/news facts | Prefer events/updates within 24 hours; up to 72 hours only with an explicit continuing development or contextual angle. Older information must be dated background, not “today's news”. | Recheck material current facts within 2 hours of approval; re-review a news draft held over 24 hours. Stable historical facts keep original dates. |
| Fixture, venue, competition, kickoff/status | Retrieve within 60 minutes; resolve both vendors' IDs to the same event, date, home/away sides and round. | Recheck within 15 minutes; postponement, venue switch or unresolved mismatch blocks publication. |
| Team news and suspension eligibility | Check **both clubs** and relevant competition discipline after their latest available pre-match update; retrieve within 6 hours; substantive update no older than 48 hours. | Recheck within 60 minutes and after new squad announcements. Material changes invalidate prior review. Unknown status is not a fresh all-clear. |
| Form/statistics | Retrieve within 24 hours; include every completed relevant match up to the snapshot. Record season, denominator, date range and competition strength. | Reject if a more recent completed match is missing or snapshot is over 24 hours old. |
| Selection odds | Request receipt **and** provider/bookmaker market update no older than 15 minutes; preserve both times. | Both no older than **5 minutes**, exact bookmaker/selection/line rechecked. Missing/unknown update time fails. No suspended market or guessed price. |

Use UTC ISO 8601 timestamps in evidence; show the user's timezone only as presentation. These windows are not promises that a source updates that often; a feed slower than a window is unsuitable for that check. Material developments always override the clock.

## 5. Blog/news draft standards

**Originality:** write a new synthesis grounded in verified facts, not sentence-by-sentence rewriting. No copied articles, translated copying, close paraphrase, distinctive borrowed structure, invented quotes, paywall bypass or unsupported claims. Identify facts from permitted evidence, then write an original angle; a human compares the result with originals before approval. Attribution does not cure copying or missing access rights.

Required package (map to [Article](../prisma/schema.prisma); these are editorial requirements, not a claim that every field is already validated):

- **Headline:** original, accurate, normally 45–80 characters; no clickbait, fabricated urgency or rumour presented as fact.
- **Summary/excerpt:** 35–60 words answering what happened and why it matters.
- **Body:** 350–650 words for routine news; 700–1,000 for explanatory analysis. A short update may be 200–350 with reviewer approval. Never pad with invented facts.
- **Structure:** dated factual lead; verified details with inline sources; football context; separately signposted **Commentary/Analysis**; what is next and what remains unknown; source list and research timestamp. Separate **Confirmed**, **Reported** and **Commentary**, with headings where useful.
- **Tags:** 2–5 canonical competition/team/topic tags; no spam, unsupported player associations or unrelated SEO terms.
- **Sources:** at least one relevant Tier A or B source for the core event; satisfy the independent-report rule for additional consequential reports. If no permissible core evidence exists, no draft. Reviewer-only provenance must not silently replace required public attribution.
- **Duplicate avoidance:** compare published, pending and rejected coverage over seven days by event/fixture ID, topic, source canonical URL and material development, not just slug/headline. Update an existing pending draft for the same story; flag a genuinely new development for reviewer-led update/follow-up, never silently change a published story. A new locale or reordered title is not a new story.
- **Images:** automation sets no third-party image/cover URL. No RSS thumbnails, hotlinked photos, badges, logos, broadcast stills or scraped graphics. Text-only is valid; a human may add an Overlay-owned graphic after documenting ownership. No fake match photos or misleading player imagery.
- **Tone:** calm, informative, respectful and adult-facing; no sensationalism, fan abuse, defamatory speculation, gambling pressure or insider-access claims. No implication of official endorsement. AI-assisted research/drafting and human review must be visible to the reviewer; public disclosure wording is a pre-launch decision.

## 6. Free-tip evidence standard

### 6.1 “Strong candidate” means **all** gates pass

“Strong” means **well-evidenced and reviewable**, not a quantified chance of winning or proof of positive expected value. This policy cannot establish profitability. The LLM supplies a qualitative assessment only.

1. **Eligible event and time:** a pre-match senior men's fixture in the explicit Phase 1 allowlist, with verified identity/competition/venue/kickoff. Generate **2–36 hours before kickoff**. Reviewer approval/publication must occur **at least 60 minutes before kickoff**; otherwise reject/expire, never rush the review.
2. **At least two independent source organisations**, including at least one contract-cleared Tier B **performance-data source**. Additionally check team news for **both teams** through current Tier A club statements or independently corroborated, licensed original reporting, and check suspension applicability. An odds quote alone is not performance evidence; a price and an unrelated club link do not meet the two-source gate. All rationale claims need supporting evidence; typical packages will have a stats provider, odds provider and both club sources.
3. **Adequate comparable data:** at least **10 completed competitive matches per team within 180 days**, including **five in the current season**. Record goals for/against, results and denominators; consider opponent/competition context, not raw cross-division comparisons. A venue-specific trend needs five relevant home/away matches in that period. Only make an xG claim if a licensed, currently adopted source supplies it. Do not manufacture absent metrics or treat missing values as zero.
4. **Market-specific reasoning:** at least two distinct supported observations, one about each team, plus one concrete opposing consideration. 1X2 must address opponent quality, home/away and the draw; BTTS must address each team's scoring **and conceding** evidence; totals must address both teams' goal environment and the exact line. Head-to-head or recent wins alone never suffice. No selection justified only by another site's tip, fame, vibes or odds shortening.
5. **Actionable recorded price:** exact named bookmaker and decimal odds greater than 1.00, selection, market, total line where applicable, quote update time, provider and receipt time; meet §4.3 freshness at drafting **and** approval. Reject unavailable/suspended prices, blends/“best odds” with no identifiable bookmaker, promotions/boosts and markets unavailable in the intended lawful audience. No assumption that displayed odds remain available.
6. **Bounded squad uncertainty:** a regular starter started at least five of the team's latest ten comparable competitive matches. A critical player is any goalkeeper tied for most starts over those ten, or a player with at least 20% of its goals over that sample (apply the scorer threshold only when the team scored at least one goal). Incomplete player/start records make this check unknown and block drafting. Unknown fitness, availability or likely rest for **any critical player**, or for **three or more regular starters on either team**, disqualifies. Verified absences may be analysed, but missing injury coverage is not verification. If the rationale depends on a particular starter/formation and it is not confirmed, reject regardless of count. Expected cup rotation without a credible squad indication disqualifies; official XIs are required if needed to resolve it. Otherwise a pre-lineup draft must flag “starting XIs not yet confirmed”; absence of an XI alone is not proof of uncertainty above threshold.
7. **No material contradiction or rights gap:** no unresolved kickoff/identity mismatch, key injury/suspension disagreement, unlicensed evidence, current-data outage or manipulated price. FA Cup qualifying rounds and the first/second rounds proper, plus any tie involving a team outside the four covered league divisions, are **excluded from tip drafting in Phase 1**, although official FA Cup news remains in scope. Other cup ties must pass every sample, comparability and rotation gate; being in a named competition is not enough.
8. **Balanced, auditable presentation:** explain why the evidence supports this informational selection and what could make it wrong; provide counterarguments, uncertainty/expiry and the unchanged disclaimer below. All sources, access status and review flags must be inspectable. Any blocking flag means no approval; zero candidates is a successful safe outcome.

**Market scope:** single selections only: 1X2, BTTS yes/no and full-match total goals over/under. Specify **90 minutes plus stoppage time, excluding extra time/penalties** and verify bookmaker rules, especially in cups. Initially use half-goal total lines only (for example 2.5), avoiding ambiguous integer/quarter-line push/split settlement. No “to qualify”, correct-score, in-play, player props, combined markets or extrapolation from an unavailable market.

| Evidence | At least two independent origins; per-source tier, publisher, origin/upstream supplier, URL/endpoint, relevant IDs, dates, permission reference and claim mapping. Both teams' form samples/denominators and snapshot times; team-news/suspension checks, known absences and unresolved statuses. |
| Analysis | Original 150–250-word rationale, at least two evidence-backed supporting observations, at least one substantive counterargument, at least two concrete risks, explicit unknowns and what would invalidate the selection. No unsupported “value bet” conclusion. |
| Review controls | `reviewerWarnings`/flags (including pre-lineup status), blocking/non-blocking classification, `expiresAt`, research time, actual human reviewer and review time only when supplied by the review action; never fabricate human sign-off. |

Store within the licensed retention allowance. Never commit source payloads, private keys or unrestricted evidence dumps to the repo or workflow logs.

### 6.3 Disqualifiers, expiry and re-review

Reject/skip if **any** gate fails, especially stale/undated odds, insufficient recent matches, unknown coverage, critical-player uncertainty, uncorroborated key reports, extra-time/market ambiguity, early-cup low data or kickoff too close. Do not replace missing evidence with model recollection or prediction API output.

- Set `expiresAt = min(kickoffAt − 60 minutes, oddsRetrievedAt + 30 minutes)`. A material team-news, fixture or market change invalidates it sooner. Expiry is a **maximum validity**, not permission to skip the five-minute odds check at approval.
- A delayed human review may fetch a fresh evidence packet, regenerate reasoning as necessary and set a new expiry **on the same draft**, but only if all gates still pass and cutoff is not reached. No automatic publication, hidden renewal, stale price carry-forward or quota reset.
- If the exact selection price moves **more than 5% relative to draft decimal odds**, the line changes, or a key fact changes, mark `REANALYSIS_REQUIRED`: regenerate/re-evaluate and require a new human review. Smaller changes still require updating quote/timestamps and explicit reviewer confirmation. A changed selection is not a silent edit.
- Suggested blocking flags: `RIGHTS_UNCLEARED`, `INSUFFICIENT_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`, `TEAM_NEWS_UNKNOWN`, `KEY_REPORT_CONFLICT`, `LOW_SAMPLE`, `ODDS_STALE`, `ODDS_OR_BOOKMAKER_MISSING`, `EVENT_MISMATCH`, `REANALYSIS_REQUIRED`, `EXPIRED`, `KICKOFF_TOO_CLOSE`. Non-blocking, but visible: `PRE_LINEUP`, `CUP_ROTATION_CHECKED`, `SMALL_VENUE_SAMPLE` (only when no venue-specific claim is made). Reviewers cannot waive blocking flags by acknowledgement; resolve the underlying failure or reject.
- On publication or later expiry, ensure expired tips are not presented as current actionable selections. They may remain clearly timestamped/expired historical information. This is a release acceptance requirement, not a claim that automated withdrawal is implemented.

### 6.4 Prohibited claims and responsible-gambling copy

- No LLM-invented win probabilities, confidence percentages, fair odds, expected returns, model calibration, simulation counts or staking sizes. Phase 1 drafts do not publish model probabilities at all. Any future probabilistic model needs separately approved, reproducible methodology, version, out-of-sample validation and calibration evidence. Historical scoring rates must be accurately labelled sample statistics, never predicted win chances.
- No “guaranteed”, “sure win”, “lock”, “banker”, “risk-free”, “can't lose”, “easy money”, “recover losses”, pressure countdowns or income promises. Do not claim positive expected value simply because decimal odds look attractive.
- No fabricated historical tips, winnings, ROI, win streaks, hit rates or implied verified track records. House free tips remain separate from paid, locked tipster picks; display performance only through a separately approved, honestly settled and complete record.
- Reuse the English free-tip disclaimer **verbatim**, from [`tips.disclaimerBody`](../apps/web/messages/en.json), as rendered by the [tips page](../apps/web/app/tips/page.tsx):

> These free tips are provided for informational purposes and are not betting advice or a guarantee of any outcome. They are separate from tipsters’ paid live picks. 18+. Please gamble responsibly.

- Preserve the site-wide wording from [`footer.disclaimer`](../apps/web/messages/en.json):

> Information only — Overlay Picks is a sports-information and analytics service. We do not accept bets, hold stakes, or operate as a bookmaker. Picks and statistics are informational only and are not betting or financial advice. 18+. Please gamble responsibly.

- Link to the [Responsible Gambling page](../apps/web/app/legal/responsible-gambling/page.tsx), whose wording includes “If you choose to bet with a third-party operator, only stake what you can afford to lose.” These disclaimers are platform copy, **not a legal exemption**. Do not target minors or vulnerable people; higher local age restrictions and advertising rules take precedence following legal review.

## 7. Admin reviewer checklist

- [ ] Apply the [Overlay Picks copy-edit skill](../.github/skills/overlay-copy-edit/SKILL.md) before approval; verify its source-link, attribution, structure, headline/excerpt/body-length, banned-meta, banned-heading and prohibited-betting checks. If a safe copy edit would alter facts, leave the text and record a reviewer warning.
- [ ] Correct allowlisted competition, season, round, fixture, teams and UTC kickoff; no duplicate topic/selection or exceeded five-draft UTC allowance.
- [ ] Every source is either contract-cleared or a catalog public feed under Consumption policy; inspect all termsNote/reviewerWarnings and the owner-accepted ToS risk. Verify attribution and links. No blocked page, full copied article, third-party image or leaked key is included.
- [ ] Open permitted original evidence, verify claim mappings and timestamps, inspect original/upstream origins, and distinguish confirmed facts, reports and commentary. Sources support the actual reasoning, not just the topic.
- [ ] Article is original, proportionate, correctly tagged, non-defamatory and materially new; summary/headline/body meet the editorial standard. No unattributed copying, invented quote or fake photo.
- [ ] Tip satisfies **every** strong-candidate gate: recent comparable samples for both teams, independent performance evidence, both clubs' team-news checks and applicable discipline/rotation review; identify unknowns, counterargument and risks.
- [ ] Exact bookmaker, market, line, 90-minute settlement scope and lawful audience verified; quote receipt and upstream update are within five minutes; fixture check within 15 minutes; current team news within 60 minutes.
- [ ] No remaining blocking flags; reanalysis completed after material price/fact changes. At least 60 minutes remain before kickoff; expiry is future and conforms to policy; stale published display/expiry controls are ready.
- [ ] No probabilities, guaranteed language, pressure selling, staking instructions, fabricated history or implied paid-tipster verification; unchanged informational/18+ disclaimer and responsible-gambling link present.
- [ ] Human reviewer identity, revision and approval time are captured by the actual admin action. A draft held beyond any window is refreshed and re-reviewed or rejected. Automation cannot publish or pretend a review occurred.
